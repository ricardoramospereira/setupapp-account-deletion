# Account and Data Deletion – Setup.app

This page explains how users can request the deletion of their account and personal data
from the **Setup.app – Ponto Digital** mobile application, in accordance with applicable
data protection laws, including the Brazilian LGPD and Google Play policies.

## How to request account deletion

To request the deletion of your account and associated personal data, please send an email to:

**support@setup.app**

Include the following information in your message:
- Full name
- Registered email address
- Company name (if applicable)
- Subject: "Account Deletion Request – Setup.app"

## Processing of requests

After receiving your request, our support team will verify the information and process the
account deletion.

Please note that certain records may be retained for a limited period when required by
legal, regulatory, or labor obligations.

Once the process is completed, access to the application will be permanently revoked.
